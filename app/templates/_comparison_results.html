Amounts are in each company's reporting currency (M = million, B = billion, T = trillion). Debt-to-equity = total debt ÷ shareholders' equity. "—" means Yahoo did not report the figure.

Analyst ratings

Source: Yahoo Finance analyst consensus. Upside = mean price target ÷ latest closing price − 1.

News
